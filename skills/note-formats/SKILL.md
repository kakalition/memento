# Note formats

Use a consistent shape so notes are easy to scan and to search later.

## Meeting notes

- **Title:** `Meeting — <topic> (<date>)`
- **Body:** attendees, decisions, and action items as short lines.
- **Tags:** `meeting` plus the project or person involved.

## Ideas

- **Title:** a one-line claim ("Idea — offload digests to a weekly job").
- **Body:** the idea, then *why* it might matter, then open questions.
- **Tags:** `idea` plus the domain.

## Tasks

- **Title:** start with a verb ("Email the vendor about pricing").
- **Body:** the outcome you want and any deadline.
- **Tags:** `task` plus a project tag.

Always prefer the owner's own words. Keep titles under ~80 characters and bodies
under a few short paragraphs; split anything longer into separate notes.
